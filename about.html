Who we are

Fire protection specialists across the Southern Region

Trimy Fire Tech Sdn. Bhd. is a fast-growing company specialising in fire protection systems and services across the Southern Region of Malaysia and Singapore.

Our core expertise is fire protection system installation and the electrical services that go with it — for factories, warehouses, high-rise buildings, power plants and more. We provide end-to-end solutions covering installation, maintenance and consultation.

Since our establishment we have expanded steadily, driven by a skilled team and a commitment to quality. Our mission is to deliver reliable, customised fire safety solutions that suit the building actually in front of us.
