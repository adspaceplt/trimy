Complete scope under one contractor

Fire protection work is commonly divided between several vendors, which leaves responsibility unclear at handover. Trimy Fire Tech covers detection, suppression and containment, together with the associated electrical works.

Detection and alarm

  • Addressable and conventional alarm panels
  • Smoke, heat and gas detection
  • VESDA aspirating smoke detection
  • Emergency exit illumination (Keluar signage)

Suppression

  • Automatic sprinkler systems
  • Hydrant and hose reel systems
  • Clean agent and CO2 extinguishing systems
  • Wet chemical systems for commercial kitchens

Containment and egress

  • Fire roller shutters and fire curtains
  • Fire doors with magnetic interlocking
  • Portable extinguishers and EFEIS renewal
  • Inspection, servicing and repressurising
