04 / Answers

Before we visit your site.

What is the difference between a fire protection system and a fire suppression system?

A fire protection system is the building wide provision: the alarm panel, detectors, sprinklers, hydrants and hose reels that detect a fire and get water to it.

A fire suppression system protects a specific area where water would cause damage, such as a server room, a commercial kitchen or a switch room. It uses CO2, a clean agent or wet chemical instead of water. Most buildings need the first. Some need both.

Do you handle the Bomba submission, or do I arrange that myself?

We handle it. Drawings, installation, testing and the submission through to the Fire Certificate are carried out by us, so you are not left coordinating between a contractor and the authority.

Can you service a system another contractor installed?

Yes. We take over maintenance of existing systems regularly, including ones that have not been serviced for some time. The first step is a site survey to record what is installed and what condition it is in.

How quickly can you attend a breakdown or a failed inspection?

Contact us on the Fire Systems line and describe the fault and the building. Priority attendance is arranged case by case depending on the severity and your location in Johor.

Which areas do you cover?

Johor. Kulai, Johor Bahru, Senai, Pasir Gudang, Iskandar Puteri and Skudai, and across the state to Batu Pahat, Muar, Segamat and Kluang.
