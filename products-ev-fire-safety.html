EV fire safety questions

Can an ordinary fire extinguisher put out an electric vehicle fire?

Not reliably. A lithium battery in thermal runaway keeps producing its own heat and reignites after the surface has been cooled.

Ordinary media can knock down flames on surrounding material, but it does not stop the cells burning. Equipment rated for the job, or a fire blanket, is what contains the incident.

What does an EV fire blanket actually do?

It is placed over the burning vehicle to smother the fire and contain the smoke. It does not extinguish the battery. What it does is hold the incident in place and limit spread to nearby vehicles and structure until the fire brigade arrives.

That matters most in a basement or a multi storey car park, where a single vehicle fire can spread quickly.

Is the EV fire blanket SIRIM approved?

Yes. The EV fire blanket we supply is SIRIM approved, and we deliver across Johor and Malaysia.

Who needs this equipment?

Car parks and basements, EV workshops and showrooms, logistics and fleet yards, and any premises with charging bays installed. It is increasingly expected wherever chargers are put in.
