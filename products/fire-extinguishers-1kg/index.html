1KG Fire Extinguisher

The compact ABC dry powder unit for a car, a motorcycle, a small office or a kitchen. Supplied with a bracket and serviced annually.

A 1KG ABC dry powder extinguisher is the smallest unit most people will ever buy, and the one that gets carried in a vehicle. It is rated for the three fire classes a driver or a small business is most likely to meet: Class A for paper, wood and fabric, Class B for petrol, diesel and solvents, and Class C for gas.

At roughly a kilogram and a half all in, it mounts inside a car boot, under a counter, or on a wall bracket in a shoplot without needing a stand. It is a first-response tool, meant for a fire still small enough to approach, not a substitute for a hose reel or a fixed system.

Where a 1KG unit is the right size

  • In a private car, a taxi or an e-hailing vehicle, where boot space is the constraint.
  • On a motorcycle or in a delivery van, mounted in the bracket supplied.
  • In a small office, a retail lot, a clinic reception or a server cupboard.
  • As a supplementary unit beside a larger extinguisher in a workshop or store.

What we supply with it

  • The extinguisher, pressure gauge and discharge hose or nozzle as supplied by the manufacturer.
  • A wall or vehicle mounting bracket.
  • A service label showing the date of supply and the date the next service falls due.

1KG Fire Extinguisher questions

Is a 1KG fire extinguisher enough for a car?

For a fire caught early — a wiring fault, a small engine bay fire, a bin fire — a 1KG unit is the size most drivers can actually reach and operate in the seconds available.

It is not sized for a fully developed vehicle fire. If the fire has spread beyond the engine bay or reached the fuel system, the correct action is to move away from the vehicle and call 999.

How often does it need servicing?

Portable extinguishers in Malaysia are normally inspected and serviced once a year, and the service record kept available for inspection.

We service what we supply and will remind you when the date falls due. Our maintenance page sets out what the visit covers.

Does it come with a SIRIM mark?

We supply extinguishers carrying the certification marks required for sale in Malaysia. The specific marks and standard references are printed on the cylinder label of the unit you receive.

If you need documentation for a Bomba submission or an audit, tell us when you order and we will supply it with the delivery.

Can I buy just one?

Yes. We supply single units to walk-in and delivery customers as well as bulk orders to contractors and facility managers.

For orders of more than ten units, message us for pricing rather than ordering one at a time.
