Full scope of works

Coverage across the four areas assessed at inspection: detection, suppression, containment and certification.

Detection and alarm

Installation, commissioning and fault diagnosis of alarm and detection systems across all building classifications.

  • Fire alarm systems, addressable and LED conventional
  • Fire detection systems
  • Smoke and heat detector systems
  • VESDA aspirating (sensitive smoke) systems
  • Gas detection
  • Emergency exit illumination, Keluar signage

Suppression and extinguishing

Water, gas and chemical systems sized to the assessed risk, installed to specification and tested for handover.

  • Automatic sprinkler systems
  • Hydrant systems
  • Hydraulic hose reel systems
  • Fire extinguishing systems including clean agent
  • CO2 systems, including panel wiring and fault diagnosis
  • Wet chemical systems for commercial kitchens

Containment and egress

Compartmentation and escape route provision, which together account for a substantial part of any inspection.

  • Fire roller shutters and fire curtain systems
  • Fire doors with automatic magnetic interlocking
  • Portable fire extinguisher supply
  • Service and inspection of fire extinguishers
  • Repressurising fire extinguishers
  • Fire extinguisher EFEIS renewal

Compliance and certification

Documentation required to convert a completed installation into a building approved for occupation.

  • Bomba submission, from drawings through to approval
  • Fire Certificate for licensing
  • Fire Certificate renewal
  • System testing and commissioning records
  • Budgeting, consulting and advance project planning
  • Fire protection engineering in partnership with specialist firms
