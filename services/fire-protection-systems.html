Fire Protection Systems

Design, installation, submission and certification of the fixed systems a building needs to be occupied and insured.

A fire protection system is not one product. It is a set of systems that have to be designed together, installed to match the approved drawings, tested as a whole and then certified before the building can be occupied. Getting any one of them wrong holds up the whole submission.

We work across that scope for factories, warehouses, shopping centres, offices and hospitals in Johor Bahru and across Johor, taking drawings through to a certified installation.

Systems we design and install

Fire alarm and detection: control panels, smoke and heat detectors, manual call points, sounders and beacons.

Automatic sprinkler systems: wet and dry pipe, pumps, valve sets and flow monitoring.

Hydrant systems: external and internal hydrants, pillar hydrants, pump rooms and water storage.

Hose reel systems and their pumps, tanks and pipework.

Portable extinguisher provision and signage, integrated with the fixed systems rather than bolted on afterwards.

Emergency lighting and exit signage on the escape routes.

How a project runs

  • Site survey and review of the architectural and mechanical drawings against the building classification.
  • System design and preparation of the drawings for submission.
  • Submission to the authority and management of the queries that come back.
  • Installation, coordinated with the main contractor's programme and the other trades.
  • Testing and commissioning of each system, then an integrated test of the whole.
  • Inspection, certification and handover, with the operation and maintenance documentation.
  • Ongoing scheduled maintenance under a service contract, which is where most of our clients stay.

Where we work

  • Factories and manufacturing plants across Kulai, Senai and the Iskandar corridor.
  • Warehouses and logistics facilities, including racked storage that changes the sprinkler design.
  • Shopping centres, retail and food and beverage premises.
  • Offices, hospitals, clinics and educational buildings.
  • Data centres and plant rooms, where the suppression approach is different and is covered on our suppression systems page.

Common questions

Do you handle the Bomba submission as well as the installation?

Yes. We prepare and lodge the submission, answer the queries that come back, and carry the drawings through to inspection and certification.

Splitting design and submission from installation across two parties is where most delays originate, because neither will own a discrepancy between the drawing and what is on the wall.

Can you take over a project another contractor started?

Frequently, yes. We survey what is installed, compare it against the approved drawings, and set out in writing what has to be corrected before the system can be tested and certified.

We will not certify work we cannot verify, so expect that report to be specific about what needs opening up.

How long does a system take?

The installation is rarely the constraint. Submission and approval timelines, and coordination with the main contractor's programme, usually set the date.

We give a programme after the survey, with the submission milestones separated from the installation ones so you can see where the risk sits.

Do you maintain the system afterwards?

Yes, and it is the larger part of our work. Fixed systems need scheduled inspection and testing to stay certified and to actually function.

Our maintenance and servicing page sets out what a contract covers.
